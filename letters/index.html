Letters to the Mayor

One letter a week, one issue at a time. Each one ends with what you can do.

Housing · Nov 12, 2026

COPA solves nothing for tenants in failing buildings

Nonprofits can already buy distressed buildings, and owners are eager to sell. What's missing is money for repairs, and COPA provides none.

Transportation, Safety · Nov 12, 2026

You'll save the horses. What about the drivers?

The Mayor is ending horse carriages to protect animals from dangerous work. Taxi drivers are dying on the job, and a safer option is being kept off our streets.

Housing · Nov 12, 2026

Restore 421-a and build an integrated city

Bloomberg's 421-a abatement put affordable apartments inside luxury towers in the city's best neighborhoods. Its replacement is building small and building elsewhere. Ask Albany to bring it back.

Schools · Nov 12, 2026

Grade the work. Tell parents the truth.

Nikole Hannah-Jones found her daughter’s wrong answers marked correct and blamed segregation and lack of funding. Schools that serve poor children well prove otherwise.

Schools · Nov 12, 2026

Stop shooting the messenger

Two Council members want to weaken the SHSAT. The test is not what is broken. It is the only part of the system honest enough to tell us that something is.
