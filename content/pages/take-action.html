Agenda item 1

Let robotaxis serve New York, safely

  • Sign the petition to Mayor Mamdani and Governor Hochul.
  • Tell your state senator and Assembly member to support safe autonomous service. It needs state law to operate.

Agenda item 2

Keep the specialized high schools and the SHSAT

  • Ask your Council member not to support the Restler-Williams plan.
  • Tell your state senator and Assembly member that you oppose repealing the Hecht-Calandra Act.
  • Volunteer at a middle school near you. If it doesn’t offer Algebra I in eighth grade, offer to tutor math.
Read the letter

Agenda item 3

Restore Bloomberg’s 421-a tax abatement

  • Send the letter to your state senator and Assembly member. 421-a is state law, and they vote on it.
  • Got an apartment through a housing lottery in a market-rate building? Tell us your story.
Read the letter

Agenda item 4

Improve our schools: grade the work and tell parents the truth

  • Volunteer to tutor reading or math. Reach out to the PTA or the principal of a public school near you.
  • Ask the Mayor to require every school to publish its curriculum and lesson plans. Under mayoral control, he can.
  • Parent of a public school student? Run for your district’s Community Education Council in spring 2027.
Read the letter

Agenda item 5

Help owners fix failing buildings

  • Own or manage a rent-stabilized building? Tell us how you stay afloat.
  • Ask your Council member to support repair loans and tax relief.
