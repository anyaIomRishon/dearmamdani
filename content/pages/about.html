About Dear Mamdani

Dear Mamdani is an independent project by New Yorkers who want City Hall judged on results: what people pay for housing, whether they can get to work safely, how their kids are learning.

Who we are

My name is Anya Levitov. I’ve worked in New York real estate for more than 20 years. I see how City Hall’s decisions and other regulations affect our housing, schools, safety, and transportation. Local politics have a direct effect on our neighborhoods and the quality of our lives. Dear Mamdani brings practical, data-driven assessments to the city’s problems and proposes solutions that work. Please join me in making the best city on Earth even better.

How we gather the numbers

Every figure on the scorecard comes from public data: NYC Open Data, the MTA, the State Labor Department, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the City Comptroller and official releases from city agencies. Each row links to its source, and the method notes explain how each measure is graded and where the numbers have limits.

A change during this term is not proof that the Mayor caused it. Many measures move with the economy, the weather, state law or the MTA. We say so where it matters.
