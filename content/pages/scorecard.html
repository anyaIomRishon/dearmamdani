How this scorecard is built
Grading
Each measure states whether a rise or a fall is good for New Yorkers. Counts and dollars use percent change; rates use the change in percentage points. Changes smaller than the measure's threshold are graded about the same. Measures marked context are shown but not graded.
Attribution
A change during this term is not proof that the Mayor caused it. Many measures move with the economy, the weather, state law or the MTA. Read each against its longer trend.
New construction
Permits count only projects filed in the same calendar year, so 2026 permits reflect projects filed under the current administration. 100% affordable projects are identified from HPD's affordable housing production list.
NYCHA vs. private
NYCHA apartments are NYCHA-managed units from the NYCHA Development Data Book (January 2025), excluding developments converted to private management. Private apartments are units in buildings with three or more apartments in MapPLUTO, minus NYCHA-owned lots. Private court-route violations count every violation at a building with a tenant repair case opened in the prior 12 months, which overstates the private figure.
Noise near NYCHA
NYCHA addresses stand in for low-income neighborhoods, since NYCHA households are low-income by definition.
Rents
The 2025 rent figures are worked back from the year-over-year change the market report gives.
Jobs
Resident labor force comes from the State Labor Department; job counts by employer come from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
